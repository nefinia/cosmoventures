Glossary Game

Words from consciousness, cosmology, quantum physics and entropy, learned one card at a time.

Browse the words as bubbles and open any one for its meaning. Choose a topic to see its words as a map, each one linked to the ideas it connects to. Switch to Cards to play: see a word and recall its meaning, or see a meaning and recall the word. Mark each card Got it or Again, and the ones you missed come back before the round ends.
